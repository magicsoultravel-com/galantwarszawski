Welcome

Galant Warszawski is a place created from a passion for tradition and respect for Polish culinary culture. Our specialty is Old Polish cuisine—authentic, exquisite, and inspired by historical recipes, which we revive in a modern interpretation.

In the elegant interior of the restaurant, we combine exceptional flavors with a high standard of service, creating an atmosphere ideal for both everyday lunches and formal dinners.

Galant Warszawski – the taste of tradition in the heart of Warsaw.
